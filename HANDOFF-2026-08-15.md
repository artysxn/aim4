**Root cause was suspect 2, with a sharper mechanism.** desireBot has two
`translator.setIntent` call sites: the full-decide one (patched through
`patchLineup(patchTrace(patchLooseBomb(...)))`) and the committed-option one
(`runner.active && !runner.mayReplace`), which re-sent the option's own intent
patched through `patchLooseBomb` ONLY. Options commit for seconds
(`MIN_COMMIT_TICKS` + latency gate) and the committed path re-sends every
decide pass, so a trace intent (and its knife ask) survived one 125 ms pass
before being stomped for the length of every commitment — exactly the failure
mode `patchLooseBomb`'s own comment warns about. This also means the old
`tapeStats` numbers were biased: the counters only ran on full decides, so
"follow 56%" was measured over ~3% of passes.

**Fix:** the committed path now applies the identical patch chain
(`shared/sim/desireBot.js`). Probe A/B on the same seed (rebuilt probe8, now at
`scripts/`-independent scratchpad; synthetic v2 tape mined from the nav graph,
spawn→site at 230 u/s, 4 rounds vs scripted): pre-contact `intent.knife` and
`knifeOut` 0.1% → 16.7% (and they now MATCH — the engine grants every ask),
all-live knifeOut 0.1% → 7.2%, pre-contact tape error 1512u → 1073u, tape
decisions counted 404 → 13,215. Remaining pre-contact knife ceiling in the
probe is tape length (synthetic tape ends ~15-20 s in) and `isOnTape` gating,
not flag loss.

**#17 groundwork: tapeEnd is now path-aware, and a re-call cannot join a
spent tape.** Two related defects found by reading the miner against the
follower:

1. `tapeEndSeconds` was waypoint/utility-only, but the miner writes waypoints
   ONLY on anchor change while the path runs to the recording's end — so any
   tape ending in a hold (every defensive tape, every pre-execute wait) had
   its `tapeEnd` far short of its path, and `isOnTape` cut the whole team off
   ('end') while `traceAt` still had answers. `tapeEndSeconds` now maxes in
   the fine path: `pathEndSeconds(role)` when hydrated/committed-full, and a
   new `pathSeconds` stamp on light sidecar entries (playbookStore counts the
   samples it strips; META_VERSION bumped 1→2 so existing sidecars on the
   data machine rebuild themselves on first open, ~seconds per file).
2. `matchSituation`/`closeMatches` scored candidates by opening shape only, so
   a post-contact re-call could pick a tape whose whole reach was already
   over at the join clock — every follower would land past tapeEnd on
   arrival and freestyle. `scoreMatches` now skips entries with no reach at
   the clock (same 10 s grace as the follower's own gate).

### The chain of fixes already landed tonight (all verified by suites)

The complaint: bots don't follow calls / tape ("move like idiots"). Root causes
found and fixed, in order:

1. **Loader could not read the 9.8 GB re-mine** — Node's 0.54 GB string cap;
   6 of 7 maps silently fell back to v1 landmark tapes.
   Fix: `server/sim/playbookStore.js` (NEW) — light sidecar + byte-offset
   hydration. `loadPlaybook` in `server/sim/bakes.js` uses it; `pickRound` /
   `matchSituation` hydrate transparently via `index.hydrate`.
   Verified byte-exact; 65 ms cold open; sidecars build at 1 GB/s.
   `scripts/sim-index-playbook.mjs` (NEW) builds sidecars; the miner now also
   builds them at the end of a mine. Sidecars skipped for files < 256 MB (the
   committed v1 `simdata/playbook` stays clean).

2. **Single-row "everyone dead" dropouts in tick buffers truncated 5.3% of
   the corpus** (17,388 roles, ~207 hours). The 2-tick path loop hit the bad
   row and `break`ed; the 32-tick waypoint loop stepped over it.
   Fix in `scripts/sim-mine-playbook.mjs`: DROPOUT_GRACE_SECONDS=1; waypoint
   loop skips, path loop holds-last-position (time is the array index, can't
   skip). Full re-mine done: **65,778 tapes, 0 truncated** (probe6 verified).
   Same bug exists in `scripts/sim-extract-bc.mjs:69` (sticky `p.dead` flag,
   2.4% of player-rounds, 1.6% of poses) — **NOT yet fixed**, fix it in the
   BC re-extract.

3. **Fidelity was unmeasured** — built `scripts/sim-tape-fidelity.mjs`
   (npm run sim:tape) + `tapeStats()` counters on the controller
   (follow %, clock error, freeze/lag/off-path split, pre/post-contact split,
   lineup acceptance). v1 baseline: follow 0.0% (landmarks offer no
   coordinates, confirming the whole mechanism was dead before tonight).

4. **Clock-chasing** — bots steered at the tape sample the wall clock named;
   equal speeds mean an initial gap never closes. Decomposed: 44% on-path
   -12.7s behind, 56% off-path. Fixes, each verified to move its number:
   - `pursuitPoint()` in `shared/sim/playbook.js`: monotonic cursor
     (`R.tapeCursor` per slot), stand-run skipping, clock as cap. Lag
     16.6s → 5.5s in probe8.
   - Spawn-aware role assignment: `assignRoles` takes `posOf`, exact
     min-distance matching of leftover roles to feet (≤5! brute force);
     `createCaller({posOf})` wired from desireBot. Pre-contact error
     1007u → 840u.
   - Knife-out model (the remaining ~15% speed deficit: pros knife-run at
     250, bots rifle-run at 215): engine `body.knifeOut`, `intent.knife`,
     `DRAW_TICKS = ticksFor(1.0) [verify]` draw delay gating `canFire`;
     translator trace case passes `move.knife`; desireBot asks for it while
     `!R.contactMade`. **This is the part that isn't engaging (see IN FLIGHT).**

5. **Metric honesty**: pre/post first-blood split added — post-contact tape
   error is a re-call problem (#17), not steering. Judge steering ONLY on
   pre-contact error. Last full numbers (before knife, noisy 8-round probe):
   PRE 840-1036u, POST 1500-2100u. Target: pre-contact ≤ ~300u
   (lookahead 1.5s × 215 u/s ≈ 320u is the natural floor).
